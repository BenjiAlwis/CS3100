## Section 2: Pair Programming
- **What was the most valuable thing your navigator caught while you were driving?**
  The most valuable thing my navigator caught was mismatches between the fx:id values in the FXML and the @FXML fields in the Controller. This would have caused the UI elements to not initialize properly at runtime.
- **What was hardest about being the navigator?**
  It was challenging when I didn’t fully understand what my partner had just implemented, since I still needed to guide decisions to understand how he was thinking about implementing our solution, as well as to think ahead about how the pieces would connect.cc
- **Did pair programming change how you think about the code compared to working alone? How?**
  I would say yes because I had to explain my reasoning out loud, which helped me catch mistakes and understand the logic more deeply. It also made me more aware of structure and clarity, since my partner needed to follow along. Compared to working alone, it felt slower at times, but the code ended up being cleaner and more correct because we caught issues earlier.
